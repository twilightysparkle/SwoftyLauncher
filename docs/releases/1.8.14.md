# 1.8.14

Swofty Launcher for F1 25 1.8.14 adds four effects to the shader library for a live TV look: a broadcast camera's tone and highlight knee, colour band targets for the sky and grass, a broadcast camera lens, and the artefacts of a compressed stream. The Broadcast Onboard config in the Addon Browser puts them together.

## Shaders

- `broadcast_camera`: exposure, white balance, black pedestal and stretch, gamma, contrast, a highlight knee that keeps colour, and an automatic knee and black level that follow sunny and overcast scenes.
- `hue_target`: two colour bands, such as the sky and the grass, each pulled towards a target hue and saturation.
- `broadcast_lens`: the picture rebuilt from a set broadcast resolution, lens softness, colour fringes towards the edges, the camera's edge detail, sensor noise and a vignette.
- `stream_encoder`: blocking, banding, motion smear and 4:2:0 colour, growing with motion.

  ```toml
  [broadcast_camera]
  knee_point = 0.82
  auto_strength = 0.6

  [stream_encoder]
  strength = 0.35
  ```

See [Shader effects](/reference/addons/shader-effects) for every option.
