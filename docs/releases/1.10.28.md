# 1.10.28

Swofty Launcher for F1 25 1.10.28 marks installed addon versions that were withdrawn from the Addon Browser, and names the ones that are on when you press **Play** and on the crash screen.

## Fixes

- An installed addon version that was withdrawn from the Addon Browser now shows **Withdrawn** on its row on the Addons page. Its panel says so and has **Turn off**. The launcher leaves it installed and doesn't turn it off. See [Withdrawn versions](/using/addons#withdrawn).
- **Play** now names the withdrawn addons that are on and says to turn them off on the Addons page. The game still starts.
- When F1 25 stays on Loading... after Press any button, or crashes, while a withdrawn addon is on, the crash screen now names it, such as **Example Addon 1.0.7 was withdrawn: turn it off**. When another known problem matches, its steps end with a line naming the withdrawn addon.
- A stall on Loading... while a withdrawn addon is on no longer counts toward moving your profile save into the career vault.

## Under the hood

- After each catalog fetch, the launcher asks the registry about each installed addon whose version the Addon Browser doesn't list, and keeps the answers in `state/withdrawn-addons.json`. The registry log, the Play log, the session log and the Send Logs summary name the withdrawn versions.
