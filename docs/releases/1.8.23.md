# 1.8.23

Swofty Launcher for F1 25 1.8.23 lets you drag the Addon Browser's category rows, brings back category tiles, and fixes four things that refused Play or looked stuck.

## Addon Browser

- Press and drag a category row, cards included, to scroll it sideways. A quick flick glides on after you let go. A drag doesn't open a card, and dragging up or down still scrolls the page.
- Category tiles are back at the top in place of the category pills, smaller than before: **All** and each category, with its icon, name and count. Select a tile to see that category; **All** returns to the rows.

## Play

- Play is no longer refused when an addon edits a resource that another addon's archive doesn't have. The edit is skipped and the build log names the addon, the archive and the resource. Turn that addon off if it doesn't work, and tell its author.
- When the launcher's own game files (`version.dll`, `swofty_core.dll`) are missing, Play now says so and names the folder, instead of saying that at most 8 tracks can be enabled at once. Restore them from your antivirus or run the installer again.
- The career check before Play no longer counts careers you deleted in game, so the number of careers that need an addon is right.
- The first Play that copies your mods to the game drive shows the copy's progress, instead of staying on **Building the mods** for minutes.
