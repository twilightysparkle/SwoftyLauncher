# 1.10.33

Swofty Launcher for F1 25 1.10.33 adds range ticks and folder ticks to the Addons list.

## Features

- Shift-click a row in the Addons list, or its tick box, to tick every row from the last row you clicked to that one. Rows that a search, a filter or a closed folder hides are not ticked. See [Turn on, turn off, move or uninstall several addons](/using/addons#turn-on-turn-off-move-or-uninstall-several-addons).
- Folders in the Addons list have a tick box while the rows show theirs. It ticks every addon in the folder, or only the ones the list shows during a search or with a filter on, and unticks them once they are all ticked. It shows a dash while only some of them are ticked. Ctrl-clicking a folder does the same.
