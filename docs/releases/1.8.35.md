# 1.8.35

Swofty Launcher for F1 25 1.8.35 fixes addons taking the wrong car in Driver Career.

## In game

- Since 1.8.33, addons that act on your car, such as AI Drive, could be told an AI car was yours at the start of a Driver Career session, and then controlled or compared against that car for the whole session. They're now given your car once the game has it under your control.

## For addon authors

- The event context's `player()` is your car only once the game's controller reports it as human-controlled; until then it's empty. Before, the core took the first car without the AI flag, which at a Driver Career session start could be an AI car ([events](/std/events)).
