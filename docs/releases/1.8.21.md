# 1.8.21

Swofty Launcher for F1 25 1.8.21 lets addons check whether the player owns the F1 2026 season content.

## For addon creators

- `content::owned("f1_2026")` in `swofty/systems/content.hpp` says whether the player owns the F1 2026 season content: `owned`, `not_owned`, or `unknown` while the game has no answer yet. The answer is the game's own, from the same check that unlocks the F1 2026 cars. See [Content ownership](/std/content#content-ownership).
- The core logs the answer once as `[entitlements] f1_2026: owned` or `not owned`, and writes it to `swofty/entitlements.json` in the game folder.
