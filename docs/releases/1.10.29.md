# 1.10.29

Swofty Launcher for F1 25 1.10.29 lets supporters manage their subscription from the **Donate** tab.

## Launcher

- With a Supporter or Supporter+ subscription, the **Donate** tab shows **Manage subscription** under **Edit your listing**. It opens Stripe's customer portal in your browser, where you can cancel at the end of the period you paid for, switch between Supporter and Supporter+, change your card and see your invoices. See [Donate](/using/donate).
- The button under your own tier now reads **Manage** and opens the portal, instead of starting a second subscription. The button under the other tier reads **Switch** and opens the portal on switching to that tier.
- After a Stripe page opens, the **Donate** tab loads again the next time you open it.
